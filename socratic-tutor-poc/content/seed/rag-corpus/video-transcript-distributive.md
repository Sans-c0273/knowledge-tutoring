The distributive property says a number outside parentheses multiplies every term inside. So 2 times the quantity x plus 3 means 2 times x plus 2 times 3 — that's 2x + 6. The most common error is multiplying only the first term: writing 2x + 3 instead of 2x + 6.

Like terms have the same variable raised to the same power. 5x and 2x are like terms; 5x and 5 are not. To combine, add or subtract the coefficients: 5x minus 2x is 3x. You cannot combine 3x and 12 — they stay separate.
